# Known Limitations

- Meta verification, approvals, ownership, and required user actions cannot be bypassed.
- Channel capabilities vary by provider policy, permission, account, region, and API version.
- WhatsApp Coexistence is eligibility dependent.
- Chatwoot Community Edition requires upgrades, PostgreSQL, Redis, workers, attachment storage, monitoring, and backups.
- Stripe and Meta may charge usage or processing fees.
- AI can be wrong; human handoff is mandatory.
- PDF and website imports require strict security controls.
- A small VPS may not support the full stack plus local inference.
- MVP covers private messages, not public comments or broadcasts.
- Legal drafts are not legal advice.
