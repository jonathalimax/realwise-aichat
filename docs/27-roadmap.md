# Roadmap

## Phase 0
Monorepo, docs, auth, tenancy, database, encryption, CI, Docker, and Chatwoot.

## Phase 1
Mock Meta, normalized messaging, WhatsApp test flow, AI state, handoff, and FAQ.

## Phase 2
Instagram, Facebook Messenger, attachments, diagnostics, and support console.

## Phase 3
Stripe, entitlements, usage, email, analytics, public site, onboarding, and pilot tools.

## Phase 4
PDF/website imports, academy template, lead qualification, trial requests, n8n, and AI evaluation.

## Phase 5
Security matrix, accessibility, load, restore, incident response, legal review, and Meta approvals.

## Later
Multiple locations, enhanced reports, more verticals, authoritative scheduling, CRM integrations, optional branded inbox, selective public-comment capabilities, provider redundancy, and dedicated infrastructure.
