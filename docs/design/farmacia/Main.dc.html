3 · Customer chat, same product
A
Alex
Atendente virtual · IA
Same layout and flows as the real-estate chat, with a man persona. Alex always says he is an AI assistant: in the welcome message and in the header badge "IA".
When a person takes over (the pharmacist answers a handoff), the chat shows "Maria S. · Farmacêutica responsável entrou na conversa", with name and role, and messages switch to that person's avatar. Same for any employee who joins.
Branding:
Each pharmacy sets primary, secondary and tertiary colours in Farmácia › Aparência, as in real estate. Default is teal. It themes the chat, the customer status page and the admin; teal is the fallback when no colours are set.
Chat screens: S1–S9 from the doc, plus S10 "person joined".
4 · Team & access: roles and what each can do

Every business registers its own staff. Five starter roles ship ready; the owner can duplicate one or build a custom role by ticking permissions per section. The sidebar only shows what the role can open. Order and handoff notifications stay on for everyone who can see them.
