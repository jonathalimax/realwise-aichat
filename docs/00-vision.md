# Product Vision

## Problem

Small local businesses receive repeated questions across WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook. Information is scattered across conversations, PDFs, social posts, spreadsheets, and the owner's memory. Replies are delayed, leads are lost, and attendants answer inconsistently.

Many automation products add setup complexity, per-agent costs, generic interfaces, weak source control, poor handoff, or vendor lock-in.

## Vision

Realwise AI Chat becomes the simplest trustworthy AI communication system for Brazilian small businesses.

The owner connects channels, enters approved business information, tests the assistant, invites attendants, and activates automation. Customers continue using familiar messaging apps. AI handles repetitive grounded questions; humans take over when needed.

## Initial vertical

- judo, jiu-jitsu, and martial-arts schools;
- dance schools;
- gyms;
- children's activities;
- tutors and educational centers;
- appointment-based local businesses.

Common intents include schedules, prices, age eligibility, address, trial classes, equipment, payment methods, and appointment requests.

## Value

### Owner
- consistent answers;
- response outside staffed hours;
- lead capture;
- one shared inbox;
- lower repetitive workload;
- control over data and automation.

### Attendant
- AI summary;
- captured lead fields;
- immediate takeover;
- multi-channel replies from Chatwoot.

### Customer
- quick answers in familiar apps;
- clear human escalation;
- no new app installation.

## Differentiation

- Realwise-owned Meta onboarding;
- self-hosted Chatwoot;
- strong tenant isolation;
- structured business settings;
- academy-first templates;
- transparent usage and controls;
- Brazilian Portuguese UX;
- optional AI providers;
- self-hosted infrastructure;
- operational and security baseline.

## Pilot success

A pilot tenant connects a real channel, adds reviewed knowledge, passes scenario tests, receives a real conversation, produces a grounded reply, creates a lead or appointment request, completes human handoff, and understands billing, limits, and provider caveats.
