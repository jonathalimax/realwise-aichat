# Pilot and Launch

## Pilot validates

Onboarding friction, channel connection, knowledge completeness, AI quality, handoff, lead/appointment outcomes, pricing, support burden, and infrastructure.

## Activation checklist

Reviewed business profile, prices, schedules, policies, channel test, assistant scenarios, handoff, team, billing/pilot entitlement, support contact, and accepted terms.

## Feedback

Track setup friction, connection failures, wrong answers, missing knowledge, handoff reasons, leads, appointments, requested features, and satisfaction separately from audit logs.

## Launch gates

No critical/high findings, restore verified, Stripe test mode verified, Meta production access approved, real channel tests passed, support/incident process ready, clear pricing disclosure, legal review, and capacity test.

## Rollout

Internal demo → one assisted pilot → small cohort → paid invite-only → controlled self-service → broader acquisition.
